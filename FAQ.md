**Last Updated:** August 13, 2026

## 🎮 ABOUT THE PROJECT

### ❓ What is BLOX 2?

BLOX 2 is a fan-made parody of Dota 2, built on the Roblox platform. It's a non-commercial project currently in active development. The demo version features one unique hero — Magma Fiend — with 4 original abilities.

---

### ❓ Is this an official Dota 2 game?

**NO.** This is a **fan-made parody** project. It is **NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Valve Corporation** or its game Dota 2. All rights to Dota 2 belong to Valve Corporation.

---

### ❓ Why "BLOX 2"?

"BLOX" comes from Roblox (block-style games), and "2" is a reference to Dota 2. The name reflects that this is a Roblox interpretation of the Dota 2 experience.

---

## 🔥 GAMEPLAY

### ❓ How many heroes are available?

Currently, the demo version features **one hero** — **Magma Fiend**. More heroes will be added in future updates.

---

### ❓ What are Magma Fiend's abilities?

| Key | Ability | Effect |
|-----|---------|--------|
| **Z** | **Fireball** | Launches a fiery orb that explodes on impact and burns enemies |
| **X** | **Eruption** | Creates an underground explosion, launching enemies into the air |
| **C** | **Blaze Dash** | Dashes forward through enemies, leaving a burning trail |
| **V** | **Inferno** | **Ultimate** — unleashes three massive explosions |

---

### ❓ Can I play with friends?

Currently, the demo is a single-player testing environment. **Multiplayer/PvP mode** is planned for future updates.

---

### ❓ Will there be more heroes?

**YES.** New heroes with unique abilities and playstyles are planned. You can suggest hero concepts in our Discord!

---

### ❓ Will there be a map with lanes, towers, and Roshan?

**YES.** A full map with 3 lanes, towers, creeps, and a Roshan boss is planned for future updates.

## 🛠️ TECHNICAL

### ❓ What programming language is used?

The project is written in **Luau** (Roblox's version of Lua) within **Roblox Studio**.

---

### ❓ How do I run the demo?

1. Download `BLOX_2_Demo.rbxl` from this repository
2. Open it in **Roblox Studio**
3. Click the **"Play"** button
4. Control Magma Fiend using keys: **Z, X, C, V**
