## 📖 About the Project

**BLOX 2** is a fan-made parody project inspired by the legendary game Dota 2, being developed on the Roblox platform.

Currently, the project is at its earliest but fully functional stage of development, representing not a full-fledged game with a map, lanes, and multiple heroes, but a carefully crafted technical demo prototype focused on creating one complete playable unit — a unique character named **Magma Fiend**.

All current work is concentrated on making this single hero as high-quality, deep, and interesting as possible, with all necessary mechanics, animations, abilities, and a progression system that will later serve as the foundation for the entire game.

This is a conscious design decision: instead of spreading efforts thin across multiple raw and unfinished characters, I decided to perfect one hero to refine all game systems, test ability balance, ensure proper server and client logic functionality, and create a solid foundation for future expansion.

The project is being developed by a single enthusiast in their free time, and creating Magma Fiend became the first and most important step toward a full-fledged game.

**Magma Fiend** is a character bundle from the Roblox Marketplace  
([Magma Fiend bundle](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/429/Magma-Fiend)).  
The visual model is a ready-made Roblox asset.  
All abilities, scripts, mechanics, animations, and game logic are entirely my own work.

This is not just a copy of an existing hero with renamed abilities — it's an independent concept with its own gameplay philosophy that will eventually become part of the larger BLOX 2 universe.

In the current version of the project, there is no map with lanes, towers, creeps, Roshan, or a shop — all of these will be added later once multiple heroes and core game mechanics are ready.

However, even in this "stripped-down" state, the project is already fully playable and demonstrates all the key aspects of the future game: hero control, ability activation and application, level and stat systems, combat mechanics, visual effects, and sound design.

This is an ideal demonstration version for testing, debugging, and gathering community feedback, as well as a solid foundation for all subsequent development stages.

## 🔥 Magma Fiend — Full Hero Description

### 📜 Lore and Appearance

**Magma Fiend** is an ancient elemental being born in the deepest volcanic caverns, where temperatures reach values that melt even the hardest stone.

According to legend, Magma Fiend was created as a result of a catastrophic eruption when two powerful magical forces — fire and earth — collided, and from this chaotic union emerged a conscious entity composed of molten magma, scorching rocks, and unbridled fury.

For centuries, it slumbered deep within the planet's core until its peace was disturbed by the intrusion of mortals who dared to awaken it through their rituals and experiments.

Now Magma Fiend has risen to the surface, burning with the desire to incinerate everything in its path and return the world to a state of primordial fiery chaos.

It recognizes neither good nor evil — only an insatiable thirst for destruction and absolute dominion over flame.

In BLOX 2, it appears as an unstoppable force capable of single-handedly turning the tide of any battle, but requiring the player to skillfully manage its fiery potential and sense the right moment for delivering devastating strikes.

> **Note:** The visual model of Magma Fiend is the official Roblox bundle  
> **[Magma Fiend](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/429/Magma-Fiend)**.  
> All abilities, scripts, animations, and game mechanics are original creations by **stakllz**.

### 🎨 Visual Design

Visually, Magma Fiend is a massive humanoid figure roughly twice the height of a standard Roblox character, completely composed of scorching rocks and flowing magma that slowly pulses and shimmers in rhythm with its movements.

The hero's body is covered in deep cracks through which bright orange and yellow streams of molten rock are visible, creating the effect of a living volcano on the verge of eruption.

Its head resembles a solidified basalt monolith with two glowing coal-like eyes that emit faint wisps of smoke with each breath.

Sharp obsidian shards protrude from the hero's back, twitching in sync with its movements, while its hands end in massive stone fists heated to white-hot intensity, leaving smoking trails on the ground.

As Magma Fiend moves, it leaves behind small puddles of molten stone that slowly cool and disappear, and when abilities are activated, its body begins to glow brightly from within, as if awakening a hidden volcanic power.

### 🎮 Role and Playstyle

Magma Fiend is designed as a versatile hero with a strong emphasis on area damage, mobility, and crowd control, making him an ideal choice for aggressive offensive tactics.

His core playstyle revolves around skillfully combining abilities: **Fireball** allows for ranged damage and engagement initiation, **Eruption** creates chaos in the center of battle and controls space, **Blaze Dash** provides incredible mobility and the ability to unexpectedly appear before enemies or escape danger, and the ultimate ability **Inferno** transforms Magma Fiend into a true destruction machine capable of burning entire enemy teams to ashes.

Thanks to this arsenal, Magma Fiend is effective against both single targets and groups of enemies, especially if he manages to properly execute his ability combinations.

However, his weakness lies in his dependence on mana and the need to precisely time each ability — if the player wastes all abilities or misjudges the situation, he quickly becomes a vulnerable target with no escape or counterattack options.

## ⚡ Magma Fiend's Abilities

Magma Fiend possesses four abilities that are fully implemented, balanced, and ready for use. All abilities were developed from scratch and thoroughly tested in various gameplay scenarios.

| Key | Ability | Description |
|-----|---------|-------------|
| **1** | **Fireball** | Launches a powerful ball of scorching flame that explodes on impact, dealing significant magical damage and burning enemies over time. |
| **2** | **Eruption** | Creates an underground volcanic explosion, launching enemies into the air, dealing massive damage, and stunning them. |
| **3** | **Blaze Dash** | Dashes forward in a burst of flame, dealing damage to enemies passed through, leaving a burning trail that slows enemies. |
| **4** | **Inferno** | **Ultimate ability** — unleashes three consecutive colossal explosions, dealing devastating damage to all enemies in range. |

### 🔴 Ability 1 — Fireball

Fireball is Magma Fiend's primary offensive ability, consisting of a powerful ball of scorching flame that the hero launches toward a selected target or in a specified direction.

When activated, Magma Fiend gathers concentrated fire energy in his right hand, which heats up to incredible temperatures, and then forcefully hurls it forward.

The fireball travels in a straight line at high speed and upon impact with the first enemy unit in its path, it explodes, dealing significant magical damage to all enemies within a small radius.

Enemies hit by Fireball are additionally set on fire: the burning effect deals extra damage over several seconds.

The cooldown for Fireball is approximately eight seconds, and mana cost is moderate.

### 🔥 Ability 2 — Eruption

Eruption is a powerful crowd control and area damage ability that allows Magma Fiend to create an underground volcanic explosion in a targeted area.

When activated, the hero forcefully strikes the ground with his searing fists, sending a pulse of fire energy deep into the earth, which bursts forth at the chosen location, causing a colossal eruption of lava and scorching rocks.

All enemy units within the affected radius take massive magical damage and are briefly launched into the air, completely losing control of their characters.

The cooldown for Eruption is quite long (around twenty seconds), making the use of this ability a strategically significant decision.

### 💨 Ability 3 — Blaze Dash

Blaze Dash is a mobility ability that drastically changes Magma Fiend's playstyle and makes him one of the most dangerous and unpredictable heroes on the battlefield.

When activated, the hero instantly transforms into a stream of scorching flame and performs a swift dash in the chosen direction over a long distance, leaving behind a burning trail that persists on the ground for several seconds.

During the dash, Magma Fiend becomes nearly invulnerable to most enemy attacks and abilities.

All enemy units that Magma Fiend passes through during his dash take significant magical damage and are briefly slowed.

The cooldown for Blaze Dash is approximately fifteen seconds.

### 🌋 Ability 4 (Ultimate) — Inferno

Inferno is Magma Fiend's ultimate ability, his trump card and most destructive weapon, capable of single-handedly turning the tide of even the most challenging battle.

When activated, Magma Fiend enters a state of absolute fiery frenzy: his body begins to rapidly heat up, massive fiery wings erupt from his back, and an incredibly powerful magical field surrounds him.

Over the course of several seconds, the hero unleashes a series of three consecutive colossal explosions of molten magma and flame, each affecting a larger area and dealing progressively greater damage.

The first explosion deals moderate damage and ignites all enemies, the second deals significantly greater damage and stuns all affected enemies, and the third unleashes devastating damage and applies a long-lasting burning effect.

During Inferno's activation, Magma Fiend receives temporary bonuses to armor and magic resistance.

However, during Inferno, Magma Fiend cannot use his other abilities, and his movement is significantly slowed.

The cooldown for Inferno is very long, approximately three minutes.

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation and Architecture

From a technical standpoint, the BLOX 2 project with Magma Fiend and his four abilities is implemented in the **Luau** programming language within **Roblox Studio**.

The code is divided into client and server parts following the classic principle that ensures security, performance, and reliability of the gameplay.

- **Client side** — Handles the display of visual effects for abilities, movement animations, hero attacks, and the player interface (health, mana, cooldown indicators).
- **Server side** — Contains all critical logic: damage calculation, application of effects (burn, slow, stun, knock-up), collision detection, cooldown management, and real-time hero state control.
- **Communication** — All client-server communication is handled through remote events, eliminating cheating and guaranteeing synchronization.

Unique particle systems were created for each ability: Fireball features a bright glowing sphere with sparks, Eruption showcases realistic lava and rock ejections, Blaze Dash includes a long fiery trail with sparks and smoke, and Inferno presents a grand spectacle of three consecutive explosions with massive particle effects.

Each ability also has its own sound design that emphasizes its power and creates an immersive battle atmosphere.
