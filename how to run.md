## 🚀 How to Run the Demo

To launch the current demo version and test Magma Fiend with his abilities, follow these steps:

1. Download the `BLOX_2_Demo.rbxl` file from this repository.
2. Open **Roblox Studio** on your computer.
3. Double-click the downloaded `.rbxl` file or open it through Roblox Studio's "Open" menu.
4. Once the project loads, click the **"Play"** button in the Roblox Studio toolbar.
5. Control Magma Fiend using keyboard and mouse (or touch input on mobile devices).
6. Activate abilities using the number keys:
   - **1** — Fireball
   - **2** — Eruption
   - **3** — Blaze Dash
   - **4** — Inferno (Ultimate)
7. Monitor health and mana indicators, test various tactical combinations, and enjoy!

## 🤝 How to Contribute to the Project

Since BLOX 2 is in its early development stage and focused on a single hero, there are many ways you can contribute:

- **Test the demo** — Play the game, find bugs, report errors, performance issues, or ability control inconveniences.
- **Suggest improvements** — Share ideas for gameplay balance, ability tweaks, visual effects, or UI design.
- **Create new heroes** — Propose concepts for future heroes: abilities, visual designs, lore, and in-game roles.
- **Design assets** — Help with models, textures, animations, or sound effects.
- **Spread the word** — Share the project with friends, on social media, and in Roblox communities.
- **Create content** — Make fan art, concept art, or gameplay videos.

To contribute, simply open the `.rbxl` file, make your changes, and share your version — I will review and incorporate the best suggestions.
